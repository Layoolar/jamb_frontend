## 1. What SabiPass is

SabiPass is a practice app for the JAMB Unified Tertiary Matriculation
Examination. You answer timed multiple-choice questions alone or against another
player.

**SabiPass is not affiliated with, endorsed by, authorised by, or connected to
the Joint Admissions and Matriculation Board (JAMB).** Nothing here is an
official JAMB product, and practising here does not guarantee any result in the
real examination.

## 2. Your account

You need an account to play. You are responsible for keeping your password
private and for what happens under your account. Tell us if you think someone
else has access.

One account per person. Do not create accounts to manipulate scores or
leaderboards.

You must be 13 or older to use SabiPass.

## 3. Fair play

Matches are timed and scored on our servers. While a question is on screen, the
app blocks screenshots and screen recording, and leaving the app costs you that
question. Two such interruptions forfeit the match. These rules are shown to you
before every match starts.

Do not attempt to extract answer keys from the app or its network traffic,
automate play, or otherwise interfere with scoring. We may suspend accounts that
do.

## 4. The questions

The app contains past examination questions circulating in public study sources,
attributed by year and subject where known, and original practice questions
written for the app.

We try hard to get answer keys right and we check every report, but we do not
guarantee that every question or explanation is free of error. **Report anything
that looks wrong** — there is a button on the review screen.

If you hold rights in any material in the app and want it removed, contact us
and we will remove it.
