SabiPass ("we", "the app") helps students practise for the JAMB Unified
Tertiary Matriculation Examination by answering timed multiple-choice questions,
alone or against another player.

This policy explains what we collect, why, and what you can do about it.

## What we collect

**Your email address.** Needed to create your account, sign you in, and let you
reset your password. If you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive your email
address from them. If you use Apple's *Hide My Email*, we only ever see the
relay address Apple gives us.

**A username you choose.** Shown to opponents. Pick anything; it does not have
to be your real name.

**Your gameplay.** Which questions you were served, which options you selected,
how long each answer took, your scores, and your win/loss record. This is what
makes the app work — it is how a duel is scored and how your review screen is
built.

**Anti-cheat signals.** During a timed question, the app records when it is sent
to the background and, on iOS, when a screenshot is taken. We store these as
flags on that match. They are used to score the match and to understand how
often it happens. **We do not read your screen, your other apps, your files, or
your keyboard.**

**A push notification token,** if you allow notifications, so we can tell you
when an opponent finishes your duel.

**Crash and error reports,** through Sentry, so we can fix what breaks. These
contain device model, OS version and a stack trace. Passwords and sign-in tokens
are stripped before anything is sent.

## What we do NOT collect

- Your location
- Your contacts
- Your photos, files, or camera
- Advertising identifiers
- Anything from other apps on your device

We do not use advertising, and we do not sell or share your data with data
brokers.

## Why we can hold this data

We process your email and gameplay because they are necessary to provide the
service you asked for. We process anti-cheat signals because a fair scoreboard
is a core part of what the app is. We process crash reports on the basis of our
legitimate interest in the app working.

## Who else sees it

- **Other players** see your username, your score, your per-question answers in
  a duel you both completed, and your win/loss record. Nothing else.
- **Service providers** who process data on our behalf: our hosting provider,
  Sentry (crash reports), Resend (password reset emails), and Expo (push
  notification delivery). They may not use your data for their own purposes.
- **Nobody else,** unless we are legally required to disclose it.

## How long we keep it

Your account data is kept until you delete your account. Match history and
answers are deleted with it. Crash reports are retained by Sentry for 90 days.

## Deleting your account

Settings → Delete account, inside the app. This permanently removes your
account, your match history, your answers, your stats and your push token. It
cannot be undone and we cannot restore it afterwards.

## Children

SabiPass is intended for candidates preparing for JAMB, typically 15 and older.
We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child
under 13 has an account, contact us and we will delete it.

## Security

Passwords are stored hashed with argon2id — we never store or can read your
actual password. Traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted with TLS.
Sign-in sessions can be revoked, and changing your password signs out every
other device.

## About the questions

SabiPass contains past examination questions circulating in public study
sources, attributed by year and subject where known, and original practice
questions. **SabiPass is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the
Joint Admissions and Matriculation Board (JAMB).** If you hold rights in
material here and want it removed, contact us and we will remove it.

## Your rights

You can access, correct, or delete your data. Most of it you can change in the
app directly. For anything else, contact us.

## Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and notify you in the app
before the change takes effect.
